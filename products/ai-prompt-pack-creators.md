## SECTION 1 — PRODUCT DESCRIPTIONS & LISTINGS (10 Prompts)

**1. Gumroad Product Description**
> "Write a compelling Gumroad product description for: [PRODUCT NAME]. It is a [TYPE: template/ebook/script/pack] that helps [TARGET CUSTOMER] achieve [OUTCOME]. Price: [PRICE]. Format it with: a one-line hook, a WHAT IT DOES section, a WHO IT'S FOR section, a HOW TO USE section, and a closing call to action. Keep it under 300 words."

**2. Bullet Point Features Writer**
> "Write 6 benefit-focused bullet points for [PRODUCT]. Each bullet should start with a strong verb and focus on what the customer gets, not just what the product contains. Target customer: [WHO THEY ARE]."

**3. Product Name Generator**
> "Generate 10 product name options for [DESCRIBE YOUR PRODUCT]. Names should be: memorable, searchable, and clearly communicate the value. Include a mix of descriptive names and brandable names."

**4. Pricing Page Copy**
> "Write a pricing section for [PRODUCT/SERVICE] with [NUMBER] tiers. Tier names and prices are: [LIST]. For each tier, write: a name, a one-line description, 3 bullet points of what's included, and who it's for. Make the middle tier feel like the obvious choice."

**5. FAQ Section Builder**
> "Create a 6-question FAQ for [PRODUCT]. Include questions that address: what exactly the product is, how to use it, what they need to use it, what happens if it doesn't work for them, refund policy, and one common objection. Answer each concisely."

**6. Before/After Product Story**
> "Write a before/after story for [PRODUCT] in this format: BEFORE (describe the frustration the customer has without the product) → AFTER (describe how their situation improves after using it). Keep it to 3-4 sentences each. Product: [DESCRIPTION]."

**7. Comparison Table Copy**
> "Write copy for a comparison table showing [PRODUCT] vs. doing it manually / vs. a competitor. Columns: Feature | Without [PRODUCT] | With [PRODUCT]. Include 6 rows covering: time, quality, cost, difficulty, consistency, and [UNIQUE FEATURE OF YOUR PRODUCT]."

**8. Urgency/Scarcity Copy**
> "Write 3 versions of urgency copy for [PRODUCT]: (1) time-limited sale version, (2) limited spots/copies version, (3) 'you've been putting this off' psychological version. Keep each under 2 sentences."

**9. Social Proof Section**
> "Write 3 fictional-but-realistic customer testimonials for [PRODUCT]. Each testimonial should: come from a different customer type, mention a specific result or use case, and sound natural — not marketing-speak. Format: Quote + Name + [Job/Context]."

**10. Product Launch Announcement**
> "Write a product launch post for [PRODUCT NAME] priced at [$X]. It's for [TARGET AUDIENCE]. The post is for [PLATFORM: Reddit/Twitter/LinkedIn]. Include a hook, 3 key benefits, and a clear link/call to action. Keep it under [WORD LIMIT]."

## SECTION 2 — SOCIAL MEDIA CONTENT (10 Prompts)

**11. Content Idea Generator**
> "Generate 20 content ideas for a [NICHE] creator on [PLATFORM]. I sell [PRODUCTS/SERVICES]. Mix formats: tips, behind-the-scenes, product showcases, personal stories, and engagement posts. I want ideas I can create quickly without expensive equipment."

**12. Twitter/X Thread Writer**
> "Write a Twitter/X thread about [TOPIC] that would appeal to [AUDIENCE]. The thread should: start with a hook tweet that makes people want to read more, have 8-10 tweets with one idea each, and end with a call to action linking to [YOUR PRODUCT/PROFILE]."

**13. Instagram Caption Writer**
> "Write 3 Instagram caption options for a post about [WHAT THE POST SHOWS]. Style: [casual/professional/motivational]. Include relevant hashtags at the end. Audience: [WHO FOLLOWS YOU]. Include a question or CTA to drive engagement."

**14. Reddit Post Writer**
> "Write a Reddit post for [SUBREDDIT] about [TOPIC]. Make it genuinely helpful and not promotional. I can mention my [PRODUCT/SERVICE] in the comments but the post itself should provide real value. Topic angle: [YOUR ANGLE]."

**15. YouTube Video Description**
> "Write a YouTube video description for a video titled: [VIDEO TITLE]. The video covers: [MAIN POINTS]. Include: a 2-sentence hook, a breakdown of timestamps, relevant keywords, links to [YOUR PRODUCT/GITHUB/SOCIAL], and a subscribe CTA. Keep it under 300 words."

**16. LinkedIn Post Writer**
> "Write a LinkedIn post about [TOPIC/STORY] that would perform well for someone in [YOUR NICHE/INDUSTRY]. Format: short punchy lines, no long paragraphs, end with a question. Tone: professional but personal. Length: 150-200 words."

**17. Repurposing Plan**
> "I created [TYPE OF CONTENT, e.g., 'a 10-minute YouTube tutorial on Google Apps Script']. Give me a plan to repurpose it into: 3 tweets, 1 LinkedIn post, 1 Reddit post, 1 Instagram carousel idea, and 1 short TikTok/Reel script. Give me the actual copy for each."

**18. Comment/Reply Templates**
> "Write 5 genuine, helpful comment templates I can use when engaging on [PLATFORM] in [NICHE]. Each should add value to a conversation, not just say 'great post'. They should subtly position me as knowledgeable without being promotional."

**19. Bio Optimizer**
> "Rewrite my [PLATFORM] bio. Current bio: [PASTE CURRENT BIO]. Make it: clear about who I help, what I offer, and why to follow me. Include a CTA. Max [CHARACTER LIMIT] characters. Platform: [PLATFORM]."

**20. Viral Hook Generator**
> "Write 10 opening hook lines for content about [TOPIC]. Make them curiosity-driven, controversial, or surprising enough that someone would stop scrolling. Audience: [YOUR AUDIENCE]."

## SECTION 3 — EMAIL MARKETING (10 Prompts)

**21. Welcome Email**
> "Write a welcome email for someone who just bought [PRODUCT] or joined [EMAIL LIST]. Include: a warm thank-you, what they can expect from you, one quick win they can get right now, and a personal note that builds connection. Tone: [casual/professional]. Length: 200-300 words."

**22. Sales Email Sequence**
> "Write a 3-email sales sequence for [PRODUCT] priced at [$X]. Email 1: value/story (no pitch). Email 2: the problem and solution. Email 3: direct offer with urgency. Target audience: [WHO]. Each email should be under 300 words."

**23. Re-engagement Email**
> "Write an email to subscribers who haven't opened in [TIMEFRAME]. The goal is to get them to re-engage or unsubscribe. Be honest, a little self-deprecating, and remind them of the value I offer. Product/niche: [YOURS]."

**24. Product Pitch Email**
> "Write a non-pushy sales email for [PRODUCT] priced at [$X]. It should lead with a relatable problem, show how [PRODUCT] solves it, include social proof, and end with a clear CTA. No sleazy tactics. Under 250 words."

**25. Subject Line Generator**
> "Generate 15 email subject lines for an email promoting [PRODUCT/TOPIC]. Include: 5 curiosity-based, 5 benefit-based, and 5 urgency-based. Flag which ones might trigger spam filters."

**26. Newsletter Edition Writer**
> "Write a short newsletter edition for [NICHE] creators. Include: one useful tip about [TOPIC], one product recommendation or resource, and one personal update in 1-2 sentences. Total length: 300-400 words. Tone: [YOUR TONE]."

**27. Abandoned Cart Email**
> "Write an email for someone who visited my Gumroad page for [PRODUCT] but didn't buy. Remind them what the product does, address the most likely objection (usually price or uncertainty), and include a limited-time offer or guarantee."

**28. Post-Purchase Follow-Up**
> "Write a follow-up email sent 3 days after someone buys [PRODUCT]. Ask how it's going, offer a helpful tip they might have missed, and invite them to share feedback or a testimonial. Mention [RELATED PRODUCT] naturally."

**29. Referral Ask Email**
> "Write an email asking happy customers of [PRODUCT] to refer a friend. Make it easy: include a suggested message they can copy-paste. Offer a small incentive if possible: [INCENTIVE OR 'no incentive, just goodwill']."

**30. Launch Countdown Email**
> "Write the final 24-hour countdown email for the launch of [PRODUCT]. Create urgency without being annoying. Remind them of the core benefit, what they'll miss if they don't act, and make the CTA extremely clear."

## SECTION 4 — CONTENT CALENDAR PLANNING (10 Prompts)

**31. Monthly Content Calendar**
> "Create a 30-day content calendar for a [NICHE] creator on [PLATFORM]. I post [X TIMES PER WEEK]. Include: content type, topic idea, and format (video/post/thread/reel) for each posting day. Align some content with [PRODUCT/SERVICE] naturally."

**32. Content Pillar Builder**
> "Help me define 4-5 content pillars for a [NICHE] creator who sells [PRODUCTS]. For each pillar, give me: the topic, why it attracts my ideal customer, and 5 specific content ideas within that pillar."

**33. Batch Content Plan**
> "I have [X] hours on [DAY] to create content for the whole week. Help me plan a batching session. I post on [PLATFORMS]. Give me a minute-by-minute schedule for the session and a list of exactly what to create."

**34. Seasonal Content Ideas**
> "Give me 10 content ideas for [NICHE] tied to [MONTH/SEASON/EVENT, e.g., 'back to school season' or 'January goal-setting']. Each idea should feel timely and naturally connect to what I sell: [YOUR PRODUCTS]."

**35. Content Recycling Plan**
> "I have these older pieces of content: [LIST THEM]. Tell me which ones are worth updating, how to update them for [YEAR], and what new format each could be repurposed into."

**36. Engagement Post Formulas**
> "Write 5 engagement post templates for [PLATFORM] in [NICHE]. Types: this or that poll, fill-in-the-blank, controversial opinion, relatable struggle, quick question. I can swap in specific details later."

**37. Trending Topic Connector**
> "The trending topic right now is [TOPIC]. Help me connect it to [MY NICHE/PRODUCT] in a way that feels natural and not forced. Give me 3 content angle ideas."

**38. Long-Form to Short-Form Breakdown**
> "I have a [BLOG POST/VIDEO/PODCAST] about [TOPIC]. Break it into 10 short-form content pieces for [PLATFORM]. Give me the hook, the key point, and the CTA for each."

**39. Story Series Planner**
> "Plan a 5-part story series for [PLATFORM] that builds toward promoting [PRODUCT]. Each part should stand alone as valuable content but collectively build toward the offer in part 5. Topic: [YOUR STORY/NICHE]."

**40. Analytics Review Prompt**
> "Here are my content analytics from the last month: [PASTE DATA OR DESCRIBE RESULTS]. Tell me: what's working, what to stop, what to double down on, and what gap I should fill. My goal is [TRAFFIC/SALES/FOLLOWERS]."

## SECTION 5 — CUSTOMER SERVICE & BUSINESS OPERATIONS (10 Prompts)

**41. Refund Request Handler**
> "Write a professional response to this refund request: '[PASTE CUSTOMER MESSAGE]'. My refund policy is: [YOUR POLICY]. The goal is to resolve it fairly, keep the customer happy if possible, and protect my business."

**42. Negative Review Response**
> "Write a response to this negative review: '[PASTE REVIEW]'. Acknowledge their frustration without being defensive, offer a solution, and show future buyers that I handle issues professionally."

**43. Custom Order Inquiry Response**
> "A potential client is asking about [CUSTOM SERVICE/ORDER]: '[PASTE THEIR MESSAGE]'. Write a reply that: clarifies what I can offer, asks the right questions to scope the work, and sets up a call or next step naturally."

**44. Price Negotiation Reply**
> "A customer asked if I can lower the price of [PRODUCT] from [$X]. Write a reply that holds the price, explains the value, and offers an alternative (like a payment plan or a different product tier) without sounding defensive."

**45. Collaboration Pitch Response**
> "I received this collaboration request: '[PASTE MESSAGE]'. Write a response that: is professional, asks the right qualifying questions, and leaves the door open without committing to anything yet."

**46. Terms & FAQ Page Writer**
> "Write a simple Terms of Use / FAQ section for a Gumroad shop selling [PRODUCT TYPES]. Cover: what's included, how to download, refund policy, allowed uses (personal use only vs. commercial), and contact information."

**47. Testimonial Request Email**
> "Write an email asking [TYPE OF CUSTOMER] to leave a review or testimonial for [PRODUCT]. Make it easy by suggesting what to write about. Offer a small bonus if relevant: [BONUS OR LEAVE BLANK]."

**48. Business Performance Review**
> "Review my digital product business this month. Here are my stats: [PASTE: revenue, units sold, top products, traffic source, refunds]. Tell me: what's working, what to fix, and my top 3 priorities for next month."

**49. Competitor Analysis**
> "Analyze this competitor's Gumroad/Etsy/product page: [PASTE URL OR DESCRIBE]. What are they doing well? What gaps could I fill? How can I position my [PRODUCT] to stand out against them?"

**50. 90-Day Growth Plan**
> "I sell [PRODUCTS] on Gumroad. My current monthly revenue is [$X]. My goal is [$Y] in 90 days. Based on this, write a realistic 90-day action plan with weekly milestones covering: product creation, marketing, audience growth, and optimization."
