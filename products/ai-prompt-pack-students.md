## SECTION 1 — ESSAY WRITING (10 Prompts)

**1. Essay Outline Generator**
> "Create a detailed essay outline for the topic: [TOPIC]. My thesis is: [THESIS STATEMENT]. The essay should be [LENGTH] words and written for a [GRADE LEVEL] student. Include an introduction with a hook, [NUMBER] body paragraphs each with a topic sentence and 2-3 supporting points, and a conclusion."

**2. Thesis Statement Builder**
> "I am writing an essay about [TOPIC] for [SUBJECT] class. My main argument is that [YOUR ROUGH IDEA]. Write 3 strong thesis statements I can choose from. Each should be one sentence, debatable, and specific."

**3. Paragraph Strengthener**
> "Here is a body paragraph I wrote: [PASTE YOUR PARAGRAPH]. Improve it by: strengthening the topic sentence, adding a specific piece of evidence or example, and making the analysis deeper. Keep my voice but make it more convincing."

**4. Introduction Hook Writer**
> "Write 3 different opening hooks for an essay about [TOPIC]. Use one of these styles: (1) a surprising statistic, (2) a thought-provoking question, (3) a brief anecdote. The audience is my [SUBJECT] teacher."

**5. Counterargument Handler**
> "My essay argues that [YOUR ARGUMENT]. Write a counterargument paragraph that: (1) fairly presents the opposing view, (2) acknowledges its validity, and (3) refutes it with evidence. This is for a [GRADE LEVEL] [SUBJECT] essay."

**6. Conclusion Writer**
> "Write a conclusion for my essay. My thesis was: [THESIS]. My 3 main points were: [POINT 1], [POINT 2], [POINT 3]. The conclusion should restate the thesis in new words, summarize the argument, and end with a broader implication or call to action."

**7. Citation Integrator**
> "I have this quote: '[PASTE QUOTE]' from [SOURCE]. Write 2-3 sentences that introduce the quote, include it naturally, and then analyze what it proves in the context of my argument: [YOUR ARGUMENT]."

**8. Transition Sentence Generator**
> "Write 3 transition sentences to connect these two paragraphs in my essay. Paragraph 1 is about: [SUMMARY OF P1]. Paragraph 2 is about: [SUMMARY OF P2]. The transitions should flow naturally and show logical connection."

**9. Essay Feedback Simulator**
> "Read my essay below and give feedback as if you are a strict [GRADE LEVEL] English teacher. Score it out of 10 for: thesis clarity, evidence use, analysis depth, structure, and grammar. Give specific suggestions for improvement. Essay: [PASTE ESSAY]"

**10. Vocabulary Upgrader**
> "Rewrite this paragraph using more sophisticated vocabulary appropriate for [GRADE LEVEL]. Do not change the meaning or argument — only upgrade the word choices. Flag any words that might seem too advanced so I can decide whether to keep them. Paragraph: [PASTE PARAGRAPH]"

## SECTION 2 — STUDYING & EXAM PREP (10 Prompts)

**11. Study Guide Creator**
> "Create a condensed study guide for [TOPIC] in [SUBJECT]. Include: key terms and definitions, the 5 most important concepts to know, common exam question types, and a quick-reference summary. Format it so I can review it in under 10 minutes."

**12. Flashcard Generator**
> "Create 20 flashcards for [TOPIC] in [SUBJECT]. Format each as: FRONT: [question or term] / BACK: [answer or definition]. Make the questions test understanding, not just memorization."

**13. Practice Exam Builder**
> "Write a 10-question practice test for [TOPIC] in [SUBJECT]. Include: 5 multiple choice, 3 short answer, and 2 long answer questions. At the end, provide an answer key with brief explanations."

**14. Concept Explainer (Simple Mode)**
> "Explain [CONCEPT] from [SUBJECT] as if I have never heard of it before. Use an analogy from everyday life. Then explain it again at a [GRADE LEVEL] level. Finally, give me 2 example problems or applications."

**15. Memory Trick Maker**
> "Create memory tricks (mnemonics, acronyms, or visual associations) to help me remember: [LIST OF FACTS/TERMS]. Make them easy to recall and a bit funny so they stick."

**16. Weak Spot Identifier**
> "I am studying for a [SUBJECT] exam on [TOPIC]. Here are the concepts I understand well: [LIST]. Here are the ones I'm unsure about: [LIST]. Create a targeted 30-minute study plan that focuses on closing my knowledge gaps."

**17. Past Exam Question Analyzer**
> "Here is a past exam question: [PASTE QUESTION]. Walk me through exactly how to answer it step by step. Explain what the question is actually asking, what a perfect answer would include, and what common mistakes students make."

**18. Summary Compressor**
> "Compress this [SUBJECT] chapter/article into a 1-page summary: [PASTE TEXT]. Keep only the most important facts, concepts, and arguments. Use bullet points and bold the key terms."

**19. The Night-Before Review**
> "I have a [SUBJECT] exam tomorrow on [TOPICS COVERED]. Give me the absolute must-know facts, formulas, and concepts — the 20% of information that covers 80% of what typically shows up on the exam."

**20. Exam Anxiety Coach**
> "I have a big [SUBJECT] exam in [TIMEFRAME] and I'm feeling overwhelmed. Give me a realistic hour-by-hour study plan for the time I have left. Also give me 3 mental strategies for staying calm during the exam."

## SECTION 3 — RESEARCH & CITATIONS (10 Prompts)

**21. Research Question Sharpener**
> "I want to write a research paper about [BROAD TOPIC]. Help me narrow it down to a specific, answerable research question. Give me 5 options ranging from broad to very narrow. I am in [GRADE LEVEL] and the paper is [LENGTH]."

**22. Source Evaluator**
> "Evaluate this source for a research paper: [PASTE URL OR ARTICLE INFO]. Assess its credibility based on: author credentials, publication type, date, bias potential, and whether it's appropriate for academic use."

**23. MLA/APA Citation Formatter**
> "Format this source in [MLA/APA/CHICAGO] citation style: Title: [TITLE] / Author: [AUTHOR] / Published: [DATE] / Website or Publisher: [SOURCE] / URL: [URL]. Give me both the in-text citation and the works cited/reference entry."

**24. Note-Taking Organizer**
> "I've collected these rough notes on [TOPIC]: [PASTE NOTES]. Organize them into logical categories, remove duplicates, and suggest which points are strong enough to use in a paper and which need more evidence."

**25. Paraphrase Assistant**
> "Paraphrase this passage so it is in my own words but retains the core meaning: [PASTE TEXT]. The result should sound like a [GRADE LEVEL] student wrote it. Do not make it too formal or too casual."

**26. Literature Review Outline**
> "I am writing a literature review on [TOPIC]. I have these sources: [LIST SOURCES BRIEFLY]. Help me organize them into themes and create an outline for the review that shows how the sources relate to each other."

**27. Argument Evidence Finder**
> "I am arguing that [YOUR ARGUMENT] in a research paper. Give me 5 types of evidence I should look for to support this claim, and suggest what kinds of sources (statistics, expert opinion, case studies, historical examples) would be most convincing."

**28. Bias Checker**
> "Read this passage and identify any signs of bias, loaded language, or missing perspectives: [PASTE TEXT]. Tell me what viewpoint the author seems to hold and what counterpoints are not being addressed."

**29. Research Summary**
> "Summarize the key findings of this article/study in plain language: [PASTE TEXT OR DESCRIBE THE STUDY]. Then tell me: (1) the main claim, (2) the evidence used, (3) the limitations, and (4) how I could use this in a paper arguing [YOUR ARGUMENT]."

**30. Bibliography Builder**
> "I used the following sources in my paper. Format them all as a [MLA/APA] bibliography/works cited page, sorted alphabetically: [PASTE YOUR LIST OF SOURCES WITH DETAILS]."

## SECTION 4 — MATH & SCIENCE (10 Prompts)

**31. Problem Solver (Step by Step)**
> "Solve this [SUBJECT] problem step by step, explaining each step in plain English so I understand the reasoning, not just the answer: [PASTE PROBLEM]."

**32. Concept → Formula Bridge**
> "Explain the concept behind this formula: [FORMULA]. Why does it work? What does each variable represent? Give me a real-world example of when you'd use it."

**33. Word Problem Decoder**
> "Break down this word problem so I know exactly what it is asking before I solve it: [PASTE PROBLEM]. Identify: what is given, what is unknown, what formula/approach to use, and any tricks or traps in the wording."

**34. Error Finder**
> "I solved this [SUBJECT] problem and got [YOUR ANSWER], but the correct answer is [CORRECT ANSWER]. Find my mistake: [PASTE YOUR WORK]. Explain exactly where I went wrong and why."

**35. Lab Report Writer**
> "Help me write the [SECTION: introduction/hypothesis/procedure/results/discussion/conclusion] for a lab report. The experiment was: [DESCRIBE EXPERIMENT]. The results were: [DESCRIBE RESULTS]. Write it in formal scientific style for [GRADE LEVEL]."

**36. Equation Practice Set**
> "Give me 5 practice problems for [SPECIFIC MATH SKILL, e.g., 'solving quadratic equations'] that are similar to the difficulty level of [GRADE LEVEL]. Include an answer key."

**37. Science Concept Visualizer**
> "Describe [SCIENCE CONCEPT] using a step-by-step visual analogy I can picture in my head. Then give me a diagram description I could draw on paper to remember it."

**38. Unit Converter / Formula Sheet**
> "Create a quick-reference formula sheet for [TOPIC, e.g., 'kinematics in Grade 11 Physics']. Include all formulas, what each variable means, and the units for each."

**39. Graph Interpreter**
> "Explain what this graph is showing and what conclusions I can draw from it: [DESCRIBE THE GRAPH — axes, shape, key points]. Then tell me what a test question about this graph might ask."

**40. Science vs. Common Misconception**
> "What is the most common misconception students have about [SCIENCE CONCEPT]? Why do people get it wrong? What is the correct understanding?"

## SECTION 5 — TIME MANAGEMENT & PLANNING (10 Prompts)

**41. Weekly Study Planner**
> "Create a weekly study schedule for me. I have these subjects: [LIST SUBJECTS]. My exams/due dates are: [LIST DEADLINES]. My free hours each day are: [E.g., Mon 4-8pm, Tue 3-7pm]. Prioritize based on urgency and difficulty."

**42. Project Breakdown**
> "I have a [TYPE] project due in [TIMEFRAME]. The requirements are: [PASTE ASSIGNMENT DETAILS]. Break it into specific daily tasks with time estimates so I don't leave it to the last minute."

**43. Procrastination Buster**
> "I keep putting off [TASK]. I have [TIMEFRAME] until it's due. Give me a no-excuses action plan that starts with the smallest possible step I can do in the next 10 minutes, then builds from there."

**44. Priority Ranker**
> "Here is everything on my to-do list this week: [PASTE YOUR LIST]. Rank these by urgency and importance. Tell me what to do today, what to do this week, and what can wait."

**45. Goal Setter**
> "I want to achieve [ACADEMIC GOAL, e.g., 'get 85% in Math this semester']. Set a SMART goal for me, break it into monthly milestones, and give me 3 weekly habits that would help me get there."

**46. Study Session Optimizer**
> "I have [X] hours to study [SUBJECT]. Design the most effective study session using evidence-based techniques (spaced repetition, active recall, Pomodoro, etc.). Include exactly what I should do in each time block."

**47. Morning Routine Designer**
> "Design a [X]-minute school morning routine that helps me arrive focused and prepared. I wake up at [TIME] and need to leave by [TIME]. Include breakfast, mental prep, and a quick review of the day's schedule."

**48. End-of-Day Review Prompt**
> "I want to do a 5-minute end-of-day review. Ask me the 5 most useful questions a student should reflect on at the end of each school day to stay on track with grades and goals."

**49. Burnout Recovery Plan**
> "I am feeling overwhelmed and burned out from school. I have [TIMEFRAME] until my next major deadline. Design a realistic recovery plan that reduces stress while still keeping me on track academically."

**50. Semester Goal Review**
> "It is [DATE] and I am [X] weeks into the semester. My original goals were: [LIST GOALS]. My current grades are: [LIST GRADES]. Assess my progress honestly and adjust my plan for the rest of the semester."

## BONUS: HOW TO GET BETTER RESULTS

- **Be specific**: The more detail in the brackets, the better the output
- **Iterate**: If the first answer isn't right, say "make it shorter", "add more examples", or "rewrite this for Grade 10 level"
- **Chain prompts**: Use one prompt's output as input for the next (e.g., use prompt #1 to make an outline, then #3 to write the intro)
- **Save your best outputs**: Keep a Google Doc of prompts that worked well for you
